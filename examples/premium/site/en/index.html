Quick answer

What is Alto Lume?

A small retreat in Brazil's Mantiqueira Mountains: six suites spread across 40 hectares of lavender, made for slow, quiet days.

The main house has the fireplace lounge, the kitchen and the breakfast veranda. The suites stand apart from one another, each with its own view: the misty valley, the lavender field or the whole mountain range from the top of the hill.

It is a family-run lodge with a local team, food made on site and house rules written down before you pay. The team speaks English and Spanish on WhatsApp.

Questions

What everyone asks.

How much does a night cost?+

From BRL 890 to BRL 1,490 per night depending on the suite, breakfast included. Rates are the same all year; stays with a Saturday or holiday night have a two-night minimum.

How do I book?+

Choose dates and a suite in the calculator and send the request on WhatsApp. We reply within two hours, 8 am to 8 pm, with the confirmation and the Pix key for the 30% deposit.

I don't have Pix. How can I pay the deposit?+

Tell us on WhatsApp and we will send a card payment link for the deposit. The balance can be paid by credit card on arrival.

How do I get there?+

By car, 2.5 hours from São Paulo, paved road all the way to the gate. Without a car, take a bus to the nearby town and we pick you up for BRL 90 each way.

Can I bring my dog?+

Yes, in the Lookout House, which has a fenced yard: up to two dogs, with up-to-date vaccinations and a BRL 80 fee per stay.

Is it cold?+

From June to August, nights drop to 2–8 °C and frost is common. Every suite has heating and down duvets. Summer afternoons are mild, with rain late in the day.
